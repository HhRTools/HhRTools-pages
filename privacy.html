Privacy Policy for Multipattern (HhRTools) Multipattern is a Fusion 360 add-in that runs entirely locally on your computer. It does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data, usage data, or design content with the publisher or any third party. The only data the add-in stores is a local settings file (settings.json) saved on your own computer within the add-in's installation folder. This file contains your last-used slider values and preferences for each pattern generator, purely for your convenience between sessions. This data never leaves your computer. If you purchase this add-in through the Autodesk Design and Make Marketplace, your purchase and contact information is processed by Autodesk in accordance with Autodesk's own privacy policy, not by HhRTools directly.
