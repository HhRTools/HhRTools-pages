Privacy Policy for Multipattern (HhRTools)

This Privacy Policy applies to both Multipattern and Multipattern Demo (collectively, "the Add-in"), versions 1.1.0 and later.

Multipattern is a Fusion 360 add-in that runs entirely locally on your computer. It does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data, usage data, telemetry, or design content with the publisher or any third party. The Add-in does not use cookies, analytics, or tracking of any kind, and does not require an internet connection to function.

Local Data Storage

The only data the add-in stores is a local settings file (settings.json) saved on your own computer in Fusion 360's default add-in data folder (%APPDATA%). This file contains your last-used slider values and preferences for each pattern generator, purely for your convenience between sessions. This data never leaves your computer, and can be deleted at any time by uninstalling the Add-in or removing the file manually.

Children's Privacy

The Add-in is a professional CAD tool and is not directed at, marketed to, or knowingly used by children under 13. No data is collected from any user, regardless of age.

Purchases and Third-Party Processing

If you purchase this add-in through the Autodesk Design and Make Marketplace, your purchase, payment, and contact information is processed by Autodesk in accordance with Autodesk's own privacy policy, not by HhRTools directly. HhRTools does not receive or have access to this information.

Changes to This Policy

If this Privacy Policy is updated, the revised version will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date below. Continued use of the Add-in after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
