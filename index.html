Multipattern – Parametric Surface Pattern Generator

Multipattern is a versatile Autodesk® Fusion® add-in designed for creating parametric surface patterns directly on any planar face or sketch boundary. Easily generate custom geometries with real-time live preview, precise margin adjustments, and instant sketch output for extrusions, cuts, or engraving.
